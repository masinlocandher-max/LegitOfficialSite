# Future customer domains

`customer.legitofficialsite.com` is reserved for a future licensed managed address. The customer does not own the apex. The acquisition handler must never receive `*.legitofficialsite.com` traffic. Its host check rejects all customer subdomains.

Current website delivery is the reusable Senzapp renderer at `/s/<slug>` on the configured app origin. Customer content and media stay scoped to a workspace; public output requires active licensed publication. No separate customer repositories are needed.

Before tenant hostnames can be offered, verify unique hostname-to-workspace mapping, domain control, TLS issuance/renewal, default unknown-host rejection, content isolation, suspension/expiry behavior and recovery. No automatic subdomain or custom-domain provisioning is implemented here.

For a customer-owned domain, retain the agreed customer registrant. Before a new domain purchase, confirm purchaser, registrant, registration/renewal costs, renewal payer, account access and transfer rights. Do not imply a free domain or ownership of a managed subdomain.
